Common Questions

Questions, answered.

What kind of brands do you work with?
DTC e-commerce, SaaS, and high-ticket service businesses. Optimal monthly ad spend $5K–$50K, though we make exceptions both directions.
How fast will I see results?
First measurable lift in week 1–2. Compounding lift in months 2–3 once new creative + landing pages are running. Most partners hit a new monthly revenue ceiling by day 90.
What does pricing look like?
Monthly retainers $3K–$15K depending on scope, separate from ad spend. 3-month minimum, then month-to-month.
Do you take percentage of ad spend?
No. Percentage-of-spend creates the wrong incentive. We charge flat retainers tied to deliverables and outcomes — our incentive is your ROAS.
Who actually runs my account?
Our founder is hands-on every account. Boutique by design — we cap our roster so every account gets senior strategy.
What if it's not working?
Transparent dashboards, weekly revenue reviews, clear KPIs from day one. After the 3-month foundation, you can leave with 30 days notice.
