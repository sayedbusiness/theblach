Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Apex Growth Corp ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have. By using apexgrowthcorp.com, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Contact details when you fill out our forms (name, email, phone, company, monthly ad spend, project details).
  • Diagnostic responses when you complete the Apex AI™ Revenue Health Diagnostic.
  • Communications when you email, call, or message us.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data via cookies and similar tech: IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed, time spent, referrer.
  • Analytics via Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, and TikTok Pixel (with your consent).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to inquiries and deliver our services.
  • Generate your personalized diagnostic via Apex AI™.
  • Send proposals, contracts, and reports to active clients.
  • Improve our website, content, and ad targeting.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. Ever.

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies for essential site function, analytics, and advertising. You can accept or deny non-essential cookies via the consent banner shown on first visit. You can change your choice anytime by clearing site data.

4. Third-Party Services

We share limited data with trusted vendors who help us operate:

  • Calendly — call scheduling
  • Formspree — form submissions
  • Google Cloud — Apex AI™ diagnostic processing
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Meta, Google, TikTok — advertising platforms

Each handles data under their own policies.

5. Data Retention

We keep contact form submissions for up to 36 months. Active client records are kept for the duration of the engagement plus 7 years for tax and legal purposes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Withdraw consent for marketing
  • Opt out of automated decision-making

Email hello@contact.apexgrowthcorp.com to exercise any of these rights.

7. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, encryption at rest) and role-based access controls. No system is 100% secure — we'll notify you if a breach affects your data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be flagged on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
